---
title: My AI Policy on this Site
---

When I started building this site I had some qualms about using AI to get it set up, though I use AI to drive development extensively at work. It’s important to me as a dev to be clear about my use of AI, particularly in something as personal as a website. So here’s an overview:

- I make aesthetic decisions. Palette, layout, fonts. Anything design-based is human-decided. I may take a palette and provide it to Claude, but I never let it make the decision on which palette to use.
- I never use AI to generate art. My site icon is something I made by hand in Procreate! It’s an (amateurish) rendering of my cat Tasha, full name Hamentashchen.
- AI writes the code. I have written HTML and CSS many times. It is, to the surprise of nobody who’s done it before, very boring. Once I’ve made decisions on layout and design, I’m fine with having AI implement it. I always review AI code for intent if not line-by-line.
