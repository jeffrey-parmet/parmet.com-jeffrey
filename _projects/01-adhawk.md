AdHawk tracks political ads on platforms including [CTV](https://adimpact.com/), [Google/YouTube](https://adstransparency.google.com/political?region=US&topic=political), [Meta (Facebook, Insta)](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=all&ad_type=political_and_issue_ads&country=SE), and [Snapchat](https://www.snap.com/political-ads).

Launched in 2022, in 2024 and 2026 AdHawk data contributed to the decision-making behind more than half of all left-of-center political ad spending including the Biden/Harris campaign, DCCC, DSCC, DGA, ProgressNow, AmericaVotes, and almost every Dem-supporting media firm under the sun.

On the AdHawk team I've touched all parts of the stack including data engineering, analytics, reporting, data science, and fullstack.

For technical folks,
- The core tool stack: Python (FastAPI, DuckDb, SQLAlchemy), React (Typescript, Chakra), AWS (ECS, S3, EC2, Amplify), Auth0, BetterStack, PostHog.
- The ETL/reporting stack: dbt, GCP (BigQuery, GCS, Cloud Run, Managed Airflow), Python (pandas, polars, duckdb, Django, FastAPI), AWS (EC2, S3), R (ggplot2, tidyverse). Not going to list every dependency, more than happy to talk shop if you reach out :).
